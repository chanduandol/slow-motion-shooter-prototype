# Slow Motion Shooter Prototype

A small 3D action-shooter prototype built around tactical slow-motion combat.

## Play the Prototype

[Play in Browser](https://suhas-chandra.itch.io/agent-binodh)

The prototype is playable directly in the browser with no installation required.

## Prototype

The prototype focuses on a simple combat loop:

### Gameplay Loop

1. Enter the combat area.
2. The player automatically moves forward through the level.
3. When enemies enter range, the camera shifts to the combat view and the crosshair appears.
4. The player must time their shots carefully and tap/click to fire.
5. The player has very little room for error; a mistimed shot can result in the enemy killing the player.
6. Hostages must be rescued while avoiding shooting them.

## Controls

- **Tap / Click** — Shoot
